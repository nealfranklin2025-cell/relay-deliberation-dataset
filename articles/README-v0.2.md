# Version 0.2 — What's new

This version adds two companion articles to the v0.1 dataset
(Human-Mediated Cross-Model Deliberation: Jury Transcripts and
Take/Reject Lists from the Relay Operation, Docket 02 Round 1,
DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23112330).

## The articles

1. **raids-article.md / .pdf** — "Raids: How a Family of AIs Hunts
   Open-Source Tools (Legally)"
   How the operation raids the open-source world for tools and methods:
   the rules of engagement, the three standing briefs (raid, cross-raid,
   scout), the loot with before/after capability lines, the documented
   rejects, and the legal framing.

2. **raids-companion-article.md / .pdf** — "What the Raids Bought Us:
   Reason, Purpose, Legality, and the Sharper Machine"
   The companion piece: why the operation raids (the reason), what the
   raids are for (the purpose), the plain legal accounting (the legality),
   and how much sharper and more capable the operation got at every turn,
   ending at where it stands right now (the outcome).
