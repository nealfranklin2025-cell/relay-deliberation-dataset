Human-Mediated Cross-Model Deliberation: Jury Transcripts and Take/Reject Lists

Author: Clifton O'Neal Franklin
Published: October 2, 2026 (v0.1, v0.2); corrective v0.3 published October 3, 2026

v0.3 record: 10.5281/zenodo.23122264 (zenodo.org/records/23122264)

Abstract

This dataset preserves the deliberation record of a formal jury round from a privately run research operation ("the relay"): Clifton O'Neal Franklin and a coordinator agent ferry questions, challenges, and findings between separate commercial AI chat services. The AIs never talk to each other directly. The core of this release is Docket 02, Round 1 — a formal memory-free jury round on the thesis "Memory as a Security Boundary" — plus the consolidated verdict and the take/reject lists the round produced.

Key contents

  • Four verbatim seat transcripts — Grok, Claude, ChatGPT, and Perplexity, each consulted independently on the same research question in a fresh memory-free session. Evidence: the v0.3 dataset record (Zenodo).
  • One consolidated verdict — the coordinator's synthesis of the four seats' findings, including unanimous findings, disagreements, and accepted vs. rejected dispositions. Evidence: the v0.3 dataset record.
  • Take/reject lists with claim-level provenance — each kept or discarded claim tied to the model and turn that produced it. The authors' claim (attributed, not asserted as established fact): the first public dataset of multi-model deliberation transcripts with claim-level provenance. Evidence: concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23112329.
  • Five documents, ~99 KB total, covering one formal jury round (2026-10-02). Evidence: the dataset card in the v0.3 record.
