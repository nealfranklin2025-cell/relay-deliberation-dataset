About

These studies are produced by a privately run research operation: Clifton O'Neal Franklin and a coordinator agent ferry questions, challenges, and findings between separate commercial AI chat services — a jury of independent AI systems deliberating on research questions. The operation's governing rule is the author's own: "I don't want to lie. I want the truth."
